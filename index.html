Research

Projects

Multi-objective transportation mode selection

A mixed-integer linear programming model that trades off cost and lead time when choosing between sea, air, and multimodal freight. The resulting plan improved lead time by 70% compared with sea freight and cut cost by 60% compared with air freight.

Published in the Asian Journal of Shipping and Logistics (2026).

MILPMulti-objective optimizationLogistics

Graph neural networks and evolutionary search for additive manufacturing

A domain-informed graph neural network predicts mechanical properties of additively manufactured parts from process parameters, and NSGA-II searches the process space for settings that improve several properties at once.

Published in Expert Systems with Applications (2026).

GNNNSGA-IIAdditive manufacturing

Physics-informed surface roughness prediction

A physics-informed neural network with transfer learning predicts surface roughness of hardened steel machined on a CNC lathe, combining machining physics with limited experimental data.

IEEE ICSET 2025.

PINNTransfer learningMachining

Supplier selection under disruption risk

A MILP model for selecting suppliers in global sourcing when some suppliers face disruption risk.

Course project, Fall 2025.
